Privacy Policy

Last updated 10 September 2026

Genie Icons is a free icon library and gallery. This page describes what the site collects, why, and how long it is kept. It covers the website only — the icon-genie npm package runs entirely on your own machine and sends nothing anywhere.

The short version. A cookie counts you once as a visitor and remembers icons you generate. Google Analytics measures how the site is used and sends your IP address and an identifier to Google. Nothing is sold, and no account is required for anything except the admin page.

Google Analytics

This site uses Google Analytics 4. It sets its own identifier and sends it, with your IP address, to Google, whose servers are outside India. IP anonymisation is on, and Google Signals and ad personalisation are both off, so the data is used to measure the site and not to build an advertising profile.

It loads without asking you first. If you would rather not be measured, a browser that blocks trackers, or Google's own opt-out add-on, will stop it. Google is a processor for this data and their handling is covered by their own privacy policy.

What stays on your device

Some things are stored in your browser and never sent anywhere: your light or dark preference, the icon colour you pick, a cache of recent searches, and a list of what you searched for, copied and downloaded. That list exists so the History panel has something to show. You can clear it from that panel, or by clearing site data for this site.

If you use your own API key in the generator, it is sent only to the provider you name and never to this site's server. It is kept in the page unless you tick Remember on this device, which stores it in your browser.

When something you type leaves the site

  • Generating an icon with the free option sends your description to this site's server and on to the model host. Nothing else about you goes with it.
  • Matching a website's theme colour sends the address you type to this site's server, which reads that page and keeps only the colours.
  • Search terms are not sent anywhere. They stay in your browser.

Where the data is held

Submissions, history and visitor counts are stored in a database in India (ap-south-1), and the site's own functions run there too. Google Analytics is the exception: that data goes to Google outside India.

Your choices

  • Clear the cookie and everything stored in your browser by clearing site data.
  • Block Google Analytics with a tracker blocker or Google's opt-out add-on.
  • Ask for the icons and history filed under your guest id to be deleted, using the contact below. Include the guest id shown in your History panel, since it is the only thing that identifies your entries.

Children

This site is not directed at children. No age is asked for and none is verified, so everything described here applies the same way to every visitor. If you are under 18, or a parent or guardian who would rather a child's activity were not measured, use the opt-out above or ask us to delete what is held using the contact below.

Your rights

Whatever your age, you can ask us to:

  • Show you what is held under your guest id, and why.
  • Correct anything wrong, such as the contributor name on an icon you submitted.
  • Delete your icons, your history and your guest id.
  • Nominate someone to exercise these rights for you if you cannot.
  • Complain, and get an answer.

Include the guest id from your History panel when you write. It is the only thing that identifies your entries, and without it we cannot find them.

Withdrawing consent

You can stop Google Analytics at any time with a tracker blocker or Google's opt-out add-on, and clear everything stored in your browser by clearing site data for this site. Withdrawing does not undo processing that already happened, and it does not remove icons already approved into the public library — ask us to delete those, using the contact below.
